Country outlines: ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson
Administrative divisions: ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces.geojson
Source repository: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/master/geojson
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Accessed 2026-09-11. Outlines simplified for display. Province polygons grouped
into first-level regions for France, Italy and Spain; existing application place
IDs retained. 193 UN members plus Palestine and the Holy See define the catalog.
Natural Earth is a cartographic snapshot, not a live authoritative boundary feed.
Vatican City and Monaco have no internal subdivisions in this supplied map.
